About Surplus2Serve

Surplus2Serve is a community-driven initiative to combat food waste and hunger simultaneously. We connect local restaurants, caterers, and food businesses with NGOs and volunteers to ensure that surplus edible food reaches those who need it most - all within a 2-hour window to maintain food safety and quality.
